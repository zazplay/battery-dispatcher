Why it matters

Electricity has two prices a day.

At noon, when every solar plant exports at once, a kilowatt-hour costs about €0.035. In the evening it climbs to €0.17 — almost five times more. A battery that only covers your own consumption misses that difference every single day.

Green — the dispatcher charges from solarBlue — it sells to the grid

How it works

Four things, every 15 minutes.

1

Reads your site

Inverters, batteries and the grid meter — live, every few seconds, over Modbus or the vendor's API.

2

Watches the market

Day-ahead and intraday prices, plus the weather forecast for tomorrow's solar production.

3

Plans the day

When to charge, when to hold, when to sell — within the reserve, cycle and export limits you set.

4

Acts on site

Sends the setpoints to your inverters and writes down every command with its reason.
